About Gentle Giant

Gentle Giant Chocolate Co. is a Philipine chocolate startup with one flagship product: the Gentle Giant Bar. It is a 45 g milk chocolate bar with a smooth sea salt caramel center,topped with white white chocolate spots that copy the whale shark's pattern. It costs about ₱45 per bar.

Our mission is to make everyday sweeter with one honest, wellmade chocolate bar that celebrates and protects the gentle giants of our seas.
